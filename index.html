Collection Overview & Curatorial Scope

This catalog documents an extraordinary twenty-six piece private assemblage of ultraviolet-reactive glassware and vintage luminous timepieces. Spanning Depression-era masterworks from the Hocking Glass Company, Jeannette Glass, Federal Glass, and Fenton Art Glass to iconic mid-century American horology from Westclox and Equity, each item is cataloged with maker attribution, pattern identification, dimensional specifications, UV spectral characteristics, and collector market valuation.
